# F001 — §3.3 ownership prohibition is a double negative and reads as a non-rule

Status: closed

Severity: high

Location:
SPEC.md §3.3 (line 51)
README.md (line 41)

## Observation

`SPEC.md` §3.3 states: "No role MUST modify another role's primary artifact or section."

Under RFC 2119 (SPEC §2), `MUST` is an absolute requirement, so the sentence literally
reads "no role is required to modify another role's primary artifact or section" — it
does not prohibit anything. The intended prohibition ("a role must not modify another
role's artifact") is inverted. The same defective phrasing appears in `README.md:41`
("No role modifies another role's primary artifact or section"), which reads as a mere
observation rather than a normative prohibition.

This weakens the ownership invariant that §13, §19, and INV-009 depend on.

## Recommendation

Rewrite §3.3 as: "A role MUST NOT modify another role's primary artifact or section."
Update `README.md` line 41 to the same wording.

## Decision

Status: accepted

Rationale:
Rewrite SPEC.md §3.3 and README.md:41 to "A role MUST NOT modify another role's primary artifact or section."
