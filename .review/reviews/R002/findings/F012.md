# F012 — Verification date is required by §12 but not machine-checked

Status: closed

Severity: low

Location:
SPEC.md §12 (lines 523–527)
tools/validate.py (INV-010, lines 453–497)

## Observation

§12 states the verification section MUST record the result, the verifier, and the
date. The validator's INV-010 checks the result and the `by` (verifier) field but
never checks that a `**Date:**` is present on a `Verified` (or `Changes Requested`)
Task. A Task can pass INV-010 with a `Verified` result and no date.

## Recommendation

Extend INV-010 (and its tests) to require a verification date for `Verified` and
`Changes Requested` Tasks, per §12.

## Decision

Status: accepted

Rationale:
Extend INV-010 to require a verification `date` for `Verified`/`Changes Requested`; add tests.
