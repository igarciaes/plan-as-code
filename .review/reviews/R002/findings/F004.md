# F004 — "Planner Decision" listed as a section but undefined in the v0.5.0 model

Status: closed

Severity: medium

Location:
SPEC.md §13.1 (line 546)
SPEC.md §15 (lines 583–591)

## Observation

§13.1 lists "Planner Decision" among Planner-owned sections, but no canonical Plan or
Task template (§6, §7) contains a "Planner Decision" section, no other section of the
spec defines it, and §15 explicitly removes Decision IDs from the protocol. §22
migrates decision records out of the canonical model. The term is a leftover from the
v0.3.x decision model and has no defined meaning or location in v0.5.0.

## Recommendation

Remove "Planner Decision" from the §13.1 Planner-owned section list. If decisions are
intended to be recorded, note where (for example narrative content in the relevant
Plan or Task) or define the section explicitly.

## Decision

Status: accepted

Rationale:
Remove the undefined "Planner Decision" entry from §13.1.
