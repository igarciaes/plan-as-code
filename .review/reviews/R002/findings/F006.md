## Observation

`SPEC.md` §2 and the §6 Plan template declare PaC v0.5.0, but the derived artifacts
still carry v0.4.0:

- examples and all conformance fixtures except the new
  `conformance/invalid/draft-plan-implementation` record `**PaC version:** v0.4.0`;
- `.plan/P003/plan.md` records `**PaC version:** v0.4.0`;
- `tools/validate.py` describes itself as the "Plan as Code v0.4.0 protocol
  invariant validator";
- `README.md` says "PaC v0.4.0 is deliberately simple" and "normative PaC v0.4.0
  specification";
- `.plan/README.md` says the repository "uses the full PaC v0.4.0 vocabulary".

Derived artifacts (examples, fixtures, validator, docs) must stay consistent with the
normative version per AGENTS.md.

## Recommendation

Update the `**PaC version:**` stamps in examples, conformance fixtures, and
`.plan/P003/plan.md`, the validator docstring, `README.md`, and `.plan/README.md` to
v0.5.0.

## Decision

Status: accepted

Rationale:
Stamp all derived artifacts (examples, conformance, `.plan/P003`, validator docstring, README.md:75/100, `.plan/README.md:31`, tests) at v0.5.0.
